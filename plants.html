Scientific Estimation Methodology & Data Transparency

Estimated CO₂ Absorption: Absorption metrics are calibrated using peer-reviewed horticultural and forestry biomass accumulation benchmarks (derived from USDA Forest Service and IPCC Good Practice Guidance models). Each estimate covers a standard 1-year annual period (365 days) for the specified species, size, and approximate age.

Scientific Disclaimer: A photographic scan or mobile digital intake does not directly measure instantaneous gas exchange or real-time physical photosynthesis rates. The scanner identifies the botanical species morphology, while the algorithm models expected annual biological sequestration under standard healthy sunlight, hydration, and soil conditions. Actual sequestration depends on irradiance, temperature, pot volume, and leaf area index.
